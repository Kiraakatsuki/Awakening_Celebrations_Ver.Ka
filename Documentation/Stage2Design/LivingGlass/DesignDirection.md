# Final direction and rationale

Living Glass is a dark spatial gallery whose art, typography and interaction share one hierarchy. Art carries emotion; glass carries identification and global actions; opaque reading regions carry sustained text. Quality must remain convincing when enhanced blur and idle motion are disabled.

The selected creative direction and Gallery Arc composition are approved for this refinement task. The [spatial addendum](SpatialAddendum/README.md) now governs Arc's A/B movement and reactive theming. Exact bounds, detailed tokens, shell placement and implementation policies remain candidates for final review. No production migration is authorized by this package. Alternate gallery studies below are retained as historical exploration.

## What converges

Living Light supplies editorial hierarchy, breathing room and six equally discoverable presences. Fluid Glass supplies the floating dock, softly lit identification plate and restrained panel expansion. Cinematic Spatial supplies window discipline and the reduction of chrome around protected media. The result is a single system, not a screen-by-screen mixture of A/B/C.

The gallery has no full-card perimeter, rarity, rank, stat, unlock treatment or exaggerated selected-character zoom. Portraits are architectural openings in a common mineral-dark field. Three subtle depth levels, a gentle arc and local performer light introduce spatial character. Names and confirmation remain predictable. An atmospheric source image is extremely subdued; it never becomes a second competing gallery.

## Gallery refinement comparison

| Refinement | Artistic strength | Interaction/composition risk | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arc | Six visible vertical artworks; balanced outer/inner depth; strongest shared exhibition identity | Needs sufficient vertical space and careful longest-name wrapping | Recommend as default at FHD |
| Constellation | More varied heights and spacing; stronger spatial rhythm without overlap | Visual scan order and equal prominence require physical trials | Retain as an optional art-direction study, not an adaptive animation mode |
| Salon | Three pairs across two rows; generous labels and shorter viewing spans | More conventional collection feel; wider crops lose some full-height presence | Preferred alternate composition for constrained widths, subject to testing |

Arc is the best balance of the brief: the six artworks are immediately legible as one collection, the hierarchy stays calm, and the spatial variation is visible without turning selection into a moving target. The constellation influence remains a secondary rhythm. The original demo's game-like selection treatment is not the layout source.

## Continuity across the product

Home is an editorial invitation with separate art and reading zones. Privacy/tutorial use a consistent reading composition. Attract opens a quiet invitation. Color Release is the Arc gallery. Focus carries the chosen artwork into a chapter window; Activation and Living Portrait reduce chrome; Release returns to readable actions; PostVideo extends reflection. Settings, assistant chooser/conversations, Help and conditional features use the same glass, type, focus and motion rules.

Performer light is contextual. The default gallery stays neutral; targeting adds one restrained accent and image clarity. Confirmation carries that identity into the chapter without recoloring every surface. Six themes share sizes, controls, route identities, keyboard behavior and media lifecycle. Proposed poetic lines are interpretive display copy, not claims about performer biographies or approved assistant knowledge.

## Acceptance intent

The direction succeeds when users discover all six presences without searching; understand pointing versus confirmation; can open and dismiss global controls predictably; read both locales over any art; and experience a quiet, continuous passage into and out of a performer chapter. Perceived smoothness and sustained frame pacing require later Unity validation. A glass shader alone cannot meet this standard.
