## Localization acceptance by family

Keep internal en/zh route keys and `AppLanguage.Chinese`; visitor-facing language is 繁體中文. Titles, controls, gesture instructions, unavailable reasons, consent, camera readiness, video placeholders and assistant errors require paired text. Text reflows; do not bind by translated text or assume identical child indices. Preserve state/focus/media across language changes; CJK IME and actual Unity font fallback remain later validation requirements.

## Coverage levels and next design step

The thirty native frames are representative, reviewable visual references; shared state/library/specification coverage accounts for every catalog record. Privacy, tutorial substeps, Attract, Release, PostVideo, Closing and exhaustive error/recovery layouts receive structural/state/localization specifications here and must be refined in the selected direction before production acceptance. This is intentionally an exploration package rather than a false claim of 104 fully approved finished screens.

Additional non-route patterns cover normal/reduced shell, modal priority, deferred capability, safe end confirmation, conditional calibration and takeaway, offline/unconfigured services and reduced-motion/effects alternatives. Their design coverage is independent of deferred functional authorization. Use `DeferredFeatureStates.csv` for bilingual state copy and `MotionStoryboard.md` for event authority.
