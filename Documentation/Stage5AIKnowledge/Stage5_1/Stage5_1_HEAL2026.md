# Stage 5.1 — HEAL 2026

JFC’s 24th edition was held July 24–26, 2026 in Jember, East Java, Indonesia. HEAL stands for **Humanity, Earth, and Life**. Gaia embodies Mother Earth. Her journey begins from an awareness of life and balance, faces a world harmed and in distress, and opens a path toward recovery through refuge, protection, reflection, renewal and cultural connection. [Official press release](https://www.jemberfashioncarnaval.com/heal/press) · [Organizer’s defile cards](https://www.jemberfashioncarnaval.com/heal) · [RRI finale](https://rri.co.id/jember/regional/2603138/grand-carnival-jfc-2026-ditutup-meriah-tuai-apresiasi-tamu-nasional)

## Eight narrative chapters

| Press-release order | Chapter | Organizer concept (paraphrased) | Exhibition journey |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
1 | Gaia | Life begins with Mother Earth and the duty to preserve natural balance. | Yes |
2 | Noise | Misinformation and social disorder obscure identity. | No |
3 | Pandemic | Health crises expose human and social vulnerability. | Yes |
4 | Sanctuary | Refuge allows reflection, balance and healing. | Yes |
5 | White Guardian | A guardian of a frozen world confronts the consequences of warming. | Yes |
6 | Conscience | Power and greed can undermine empathy and moral judgment. | Yes |
7 | Re-Birth | Unity and repair make renewal possible. | Yes |
8 | Roots | Ancestral cultures and knowledge help sustain a future relationship with nature. | No |

The press release uses **White Guardian** and the HEAL concept card uses **The White Guardian**. Street coverage sometimes writes **Rebirth**. Reports describe Raja Ampat marine imagery for Gaia, mask imagery for Noise, a gladiator figure for Conscience, a Japanese repair philosophy for Re-Birth, and Minangkabau influences for Roots. These refer to reported festival presentations. The records carry separate interpretations of each chapter’s relationship to Gaia. ## Observed programming and staging

- July 24 opened with World Kids Carnival and Pets Carnival. [Jember Regency opening report](https://ppid.jemberkab.go.id/berita/anak-anak-dan-hewan-peliharaan-jadi-bintang-hari-pertama-jfc-2026-20260724)
- July 25 included Wonderful Archipelago Carnival Indonesia (WACI), with a closing performance by Novia Bachmid, and an evening Artwear Carnival. At Artwear, Firsta Yufi Amarta Putri modelled a Raja Ampat-themed design by Delinda of Domi Jolie. That Artwear credit does not establish authorship for the similarly themed Gaia defile or exhibition portrait. [RRI WACI](https://rri.co.id/jember/regional/2598145/novia-bachmid-meriahkan-penutupan-waci-jfc-2026) · [RRI Artwear](https://rri.co.id/jember/regional/2603132/artwear-jfc-2026-jadi-panggung-kreativitas-desainer-lokal)
- July 26 closed with Grand Carnival. The **Dewi Bumi** stage prologue centered on Gaia. **Roots** led the ensuing street parade, which ran approximately 3.6 km from Central Park Jember to GOR PKPSO Kaliwates. The published schedule lists a distinct sequence from the press-release narrative. The complete minute-by-minute order has not been independently audited.
