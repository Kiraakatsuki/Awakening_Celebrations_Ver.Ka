# Stage 5.1 — JFC history and editions

Designer and educator Dynand Fariz developed JFC from fashion-house experiments into a public street carnival. The first public edition was held January 1, 2003, and another followed in August. Early sources distinguish the costume groups from the later practice of naming the whole edition. JFC describes participants as makers and performers of their own work. A Jember government roadshow also documents community instruction in modelling and costume creation. [JFC organizer](https://www.jemberfashioncarnaval.com/) · [Contemporary early history](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2011/07/30/toward-global-recognition) · [Sukorambi roadshow](https://ppid.jemberkab.go.id/index.php/berita/jfc-roadshow-to-timelapse-di-kecamatan-sukorambi)

2004 organizational accounts use different names for the formal carnival body. The timeline records the change without asserting the date of legal incorporation. It also tracks the first-decade retrospective in 2011, international activity in Korea in 2015 and Seychelles in 2016, Jember’s carnival-city recognition in 2017, the death of Fariz and leadership succession in 2019, the 2020 virtual program, the limited 2021 return and the fully outdoor Grand Carnival in 2022. [Founding and succession account](https://www.detik.com/jatim/wisata/d-6216237/sejarah-jember-fashion-carnaval-pentas-sederhana-jadi-karnaval-kelas-dunia) · [Tourism archive](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/travel-ideas/culture/jember-fashion-carnival-2019-the-tribal-grandeur) · [Outdoor return](https://www.jemberkab.go.id/semarak-grand-carnival-jfc-ke-20-the-legacy-menparekraf-jfc-akan-dimasukkan-ke-kalender-wisata-internasional/)

The 2011 spelling **Eyes on Triumph** follows contemporary reporting.
