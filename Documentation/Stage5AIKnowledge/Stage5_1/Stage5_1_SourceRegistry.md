## Evidence use

- Use organizer pages for official wording, defile names and the meaning intended by JFC. Press release forecasts and promotional rankings do not establish what happened.
- Use government and first-hand reporting for dated programming and observed outcomes. Cite the reporter for named appearances or visual descriptions. Publisher timestamps are distinct from event dates.
- Use contemporary accounts to resolve title variants where supported. Use retrospective research for gaps, mark conflicts, and keep early defile lists separate from umbrella themes.
- Store source metadata and independently authored factual notes only. The raw articles, photographs, captions and press material stay at their sources. Any later crawling, copying, translation of lengthy excerpts, or ingestion of restricted publisher material requires separate rights review. This corpus uses original summaries and short names, not a mirror.
- The repository source proves which local artwork is displayed. It provides no photographer, designer, cast or souvenir rights clearance.
