# Stage 5.1 — Bilingual knowledge policy

Use `en` and `zh-TW` in knowledge data. Unity’s current internal locale is `AppLanguage.Chinese`, and saved locale preferences use `zh`. The Stage 5.2 adapter should map that to `zh-TW` at the new boundary. Preserve visitor language switching and support a clearly requested response language. Treat interface locale as the default when a question mixes languages.

Keep publisher titles and Indonesian proper nouns in the registry. The translations here are exhibition glosses. A guide can show the original title first and then a natural explanation in the visitor’s language.

Answer plainly and attribute the level of evidence. “JFC presents…” introduces organizer symbolism. “RRI reported…” introduces an observed festival claim. “One way to read it…” introduces a curatorial connection. “The designer has not been confirmed” is the appropriate response when the asset only establishes what the visitor sees. In Traditional Chinese, use「主辦方將它呈現為……」、「RRI 報導……」、「從展覽的角度來看……」and「目前尚未核實設計者」respectively. Keep answers warm and specific and give a short cited answer before expanding.

Historical spellings stay searchable as aliases. Preserve names such as Eyes on Triumph and Jember Fashion Carnaval in both locales. Avoid swapping culturally specific forms for generic equivalents. Case, punctuation and full-width forms can be normalized during search, with canonical names preserved for display. A bilingual record is editorially reviewed on both sides before publishing; structural validation checks coverage and cannot establish translation quality by itself.
